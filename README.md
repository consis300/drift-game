# Sideways: JDM Drift

A 3D drifting game for phones, made with three.js. It runs in a web browser, with nothing to install.

- **21 cars:** a 2020 Lexus IS300 F Sport (3.5L V6), AE86 Trueno, Silvia S13 and S15, 180SX, RX-7 FD, RX-8, MX-5 NA, Skyline GT-R R32, R33 and R34, 350Z, Supra A80, Chaser JZX100, GT86, S2000, NSX NA1, Civic Type R EK9, Integra Type R DC2, Lancer Evo IX and Impreza WRX STI
- **Pop the hood:** every car has a modeled engine bay, and turbo, intake and intercooler upgrades show up in it
- **Tracks:** a club circuit, Tokyo docks at night, a mountain pass, a desert bowl and a frozen lake
- **Garage:** 12 performance upgrades and 3 exhausts (Sport, Titanium and Straight Pipe), each louder and stronger than the last. Visual parts include paint finishes, body kits up to wide body, 4 wings, vented and carbon hoods, 5 wheel styles, wheel sizes, ride height, camber, liveries, headlight colors, tint, smoke and underglow. You can preview any part on the car before you buy it
- **Effects:** tire smoke, tire marks, burnouts, and engine sounds generated in the browser that follow rpm and gear
- **Touch controls:** steering arrows, gas, brake and e-brake

## Play
Open `index.html` in a browser. It needs an internet connection to load three.js. You can also turn on GitHub Pages for this repo and open it on your phone.

Keyboard controls: arrow keys or WASD to drive, Space for the e-brake, C to change the camera, P to pause.
